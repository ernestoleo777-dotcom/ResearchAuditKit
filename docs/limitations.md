# Limitations

- Policies are user-authored and can omit important assets.
- Hashes establish byte identity only.
- Empirical support is not a physical or causal property.
- Pareto audits accept the supplied objectives and directions; they do not validate response truth.
- Metadata-only leakage analysis cannot observe undocumented processing.
- Gate thresholds can be poorly chosen even when evaluated correctly.
- The package cannot replace domain review, experimental replication, security review, or peer review.

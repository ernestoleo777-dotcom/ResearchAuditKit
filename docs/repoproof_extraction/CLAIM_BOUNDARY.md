# Claim Boundary

This plan concerns general custody and isolation mechanics only.

- A prediction seal can show that a supplied declaration matches the sealed bytes. It cannot show that the declaration was scientifically sound, externally time-prior to an outcome, independently authored, or correct.
- An isolation audit can show that declared local paths satisfy a structural policy. It cannot prove human separation, OS access enforcement, no prior access, no undocumented copy, or blind-study validity.
- An evidence index can show that records are well-formed and role-labelled. It cannot adjudicate evidence, validate a claim, establish provenance beyond supplied records, or establish scientific truth.
- Existing inventory, freeze, verify, support, split, Pareto, and gate outputs retain their current documented limits. No status in this plan upgrades any existing result.

The implementation must not use the closed archive's scientific outputs, metrics, entities, names, roles, fixture identifiers, protocol, schema, evaluator, or historical results. ResearchAuditKit remains an independent engineering toolkit, not a successor, restoration, benchmark, or paper artifact.

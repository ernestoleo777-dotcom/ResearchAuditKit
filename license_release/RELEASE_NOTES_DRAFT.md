# ResearchAuditKit 0.1.0 Release Candidate

ResearchAuditKit provides local repository-integrity inventory and verification, versioned baselines, empirical joint-support audits, Pareto contamination diagnostics, split/leakage metadata audits, governance gates, claim matrices, protocol-deviation records, negative-result preservation, a local CLI, and synthetic examples.

This candidate adds Apache-2.0 licensing and packaging metadata. It remains a local engineering tool: it does not establish scientific truth, physical feasibility, complete leakage detection, or publication acceptance.
